Welcome to SWARGHAM TRUST

SWARGHAM TRUST was started in the year 2005 with a good ambition to uplift downtrodden and weaker sections of the society, bringing them to a level of good living where they can join the mainstream community.

It is a well-known fact that many people in remote areas are not aware of basic requirements. The main factors which govern the society are Ignorance, Poverty, and Social conditions. We strive to provide better understanding of Education, Health & Hygiene, and Agriculture, with a special focus on empowering women who have been suppressed for centuries.

Our Vision, Mission & Objectives

The guiding principles that drive our organization forward every day.

Vision

The SWARGHAM Trust visualizes an egalitarian civil society free from exploitation, oppression that involves all section of people irrespective of caste creed religion in the holistic development of the people, for the people, by the people.

Mission

To strengthen the struggles of rural, poor, oppressed, exploited people of under privileged backward communities for their empowerment and survival by developing alternative means of livelihood with full access to education, health care, shelter, safe drinking water and sanitation.

Objectives:

To promote the interests and all-round welfare of all the socially educationally and economically downtrodden people of India;
To conduct periodic lectures, meetings, conferences, training programmes, seminars and surveys on matters of interest to national and International weaker sections of the people;
To provide a medium for the exchange of information about the activities, objectives and experience of similar organisations;
To enable welfare organisations to formulate and carry out common programmes;
To provide appropriate services for socially, educationally and economically weaker sections of the people which will not prejudice self-reliance;
To encourage the spirit of voluntarism by revitalising the existing voluntary social welfare groups particularly local action oriented groups;
SWARGHAM Trust will provide entrepreneur skills to enough women in income generation activities to increase their income levels. Through which they will involve in climate changes activities and health care measures of children's;
They will be enough to work for child labour elimination regular schooling for children and product them from communicable diseases;
To ensure the child rights through "education to all" perception and develop the children in primary and higher education, technical education and skills;

Activities of our Organization

Explore the various camps and training programs we conduct across Tamil Nadu.

Multi Skill Training

We provide skill development training across major centers including Kumbakonam, Sivagangai, Nagerkoil, Madurai, and Salem to empower communities.

Health Awareness Camp

Imparting SHG health training to women to spread awareness on health and hygiene practices, significantly improving living conditions and productivity.

HIV/AIDS Awareness

Conducting camps in Government schools to educate students on the prevention, impact, and treatment of HIV/AIDS, along with the ill-effects of drugs.

Special Awareness Program

Focused on Women Empowerment by providing income-generating training, basic education, and awareness programs about women's rights.

General Medical Camp

Organizing 'free for all' medical camps in remote villages to provide healthcare services, catering especially to pregnant women, children, and the elderly.
